Find the existing round doughnut selector in the header and add this class:

className="doughnutSelector"

Example:

<header className="top">
  <a className="logo" href="/">JTB</a>
  <div className="brandText">
    <b>Joachim Teaching Board</b>
    <small>Interactive Grades 8-9</small>
  </div>

  <button className="doughnutSelector" aria-label="Open selector">
    ...existing doughnut selector...
  </button>

  <div className="menuSlot">
    <HamburgerMenu />
  </div>
</header>

Do not use position: fixed. The selector remains inside the header, but margin-left: auto pushes it to the far right and creates a clear space after the name.
