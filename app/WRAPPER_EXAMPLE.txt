Find the wrapper surrounding the doughnut chart and add this class:

<div className="doughnutTopRight">
  <Doughnut data={data} options={options} />
</div>

If a wrapper already exists, keep the wrapper and change only its className:

<div className="existingClass doughnutTopRight">
  ...existing doughnut chart...
</div>

The chart will remain fixed at the far top-right on phones, tablets, laptops, desktops and interactive classroom displays.
